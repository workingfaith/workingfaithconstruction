Questions

Carpentry FAQ

Do you replace baseboards after water damage?

Yes. Swollen or stained baseboards are pulled, replaced, caulked and painted to match the rest of the room.

Can you install interior doors?

Yes. We replace and install interior doors, including casing, hardware and adjustments.

Do you repair siding?

Yes. Rotted siding and trim can be replaced and painted. See the before-and-after on this page.

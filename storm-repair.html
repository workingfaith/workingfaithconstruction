The playbook

Everything inside, after the storm.

The same work you saw in the reel, done by a licensed, insured local crew.

  1. 01

    Ceilings

    Water-stained or caved-in ceilings cut out, rehung and finished.

  2. 02

    Drywall

    Wet or damaged drywall removed, replaced, taped and mudded.

  3. 03

    Texture

    Ceiling and wall texture matched so the repair disappears.

  4. 04

    Paint

    Primer and paint on walls, ceilings and trim, rooms finished clean.

  5. 05

    Trim & baseboards

    Swollen trim and baseboards pulled and replaced.

  6. 06

    Floors

    Damaged plank and tile flooring torn out and replaced.

We don't do roofing. Get the roof patched or dried in first, then call us for everything inside. If you're not sure where to start, call anyway and we'll tell you.

Questions

Painting FAQ

Do you do faux finishes?

Yes. Sonny does artistic faux finishes and specialty textures, like the faux bronze corbel on this page.

Can you paint my kitchen cabinets?

Yes. Cabinets are cleaned, sanded, primed and painted. We'll talk through colors and sheen at the walkthrough.

Do you fix the walls before painting?

Yes. Drywall is one of our licensed trades, so holes, cracks and dings get repaired before the paint goes on.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you work on houses or just condos?

Both. Working Faith Construction works on single-family homes, townhomes, condos and rental properties.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The walkthrough and the written estimate cost nothing, and you're under no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Working Faith Construction is licensed in drywall, painting, carpentry, flooring and handyman work, and carries general liability insurance. A certificate can go straight to your condo association.

Which areas do you serve?

Pensacola, Pace, Milton and Perdido Key, plus Pensacola Beach, Gulf Breeze and Beulah.

Do I need to be there during the work?

No. Most condo owners leave a lockbox code or a key at the front desk and follow along through progress photos.

Will you take a small job?

Yes. One wall, a drywall patch or a door that won't latch. No job is too small or too big.

When is the best time to book a condo remodel?

The off-season, roughly October through early March, when units sit empty and buildings are quieter.
